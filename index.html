Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you place a custom order

Can I order a single custom T-shirt?

Yes. PrintsPublic supports single-piece as well as bulk custom orders.

How do I send my photo, logo or design?

Use the “Send Your Design” or WhatsApp button and attach your artwork in the WhatsApp chat.

Do you take bulk, team and corporate orders?

Yes. Share the product, quantity, sizes and delivery city on WhatsApp so the requirement can be discussed.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, PAN India delivery is available.

What can I get customized?

Custom T-shirts, oversized T-shirts, hoodies, jerseys, polos, couple T-shirts, DTF printing and other listed products can be explored from the shop and category links.

How do I confirm the right product and size?

Open the product page or send the product name, required sizes and quantity on WhatsApp before confirming the order.
